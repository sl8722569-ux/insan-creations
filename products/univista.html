UniVista

UniVista is a universal camera, AI, security, recording, storage, smart-light, alarm, automation, mobile, PC, TV and web platform. It adapts to hardware instead of forcing every camera to behave identically. Principle: your camera does not have to become a UniVista camera — UniVista should learn how to work with your camera. This is Early Access (Blueprint v1.2, Chapter 75).
