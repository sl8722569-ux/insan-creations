How to use https://insancreations.com/ and Google verification

The studio site is ready for the custom domain. Buying the name, pointing DNS, and Google’s company/app checks have to be done in your accounts — nobody else can finish those forms for you.

1. Buy the domain

  1. Go to a registrar (Namecheap, Google Domains/Squarespace, Cloudflare, GoDaddy).
  2. Search for insancreations.com and purchase it (you pay yearly).
  3. Sign in with the email you want as company contact (e.g. sl8722569@gmail.com).

2. Point DNS at this site (GitHub Pages)

In the domain’s DNS settings add:

  • A record @ → 185.199.108.153
  • A record @ → 185.199.109.153
  • A record @ → 185.199.110.153
  • A record @ → 185.199.111.153
  • CNAME www → sl8722569-ux.github.io

Wait 15 minutes to 24 hours. Then https://insancreations.com/ should open this studio.

3. Google — websites

  1. Open Google Search Console
  2. Add property: https://insancreations.com/
  3. Verify with a DNS TXT record (Google shows the exact value) or an HTML file Google gives you — put that file in this repo’s root and tell us to publish it.
  4. Also add https://sl8722569-ux.github.io/insan-creations/ until the custom domain is live.

4. Google — company (Business Profile)

  1. Open Google Business Profile
  2. Create “INSAN CREATIONS”
  3. Website: https://insancreations.com/
  4. Complete address/phone. Google often mails a postcard code — only the person at that address can finish verification.

5. Google — apps (Play Store)

  1. Open Google Play Console
  2. Pay the one-time developer fee (set by Google, currently around USD 25).
  3. Organization name: INSAN CREATIONS
  4. A native APK must exist before an app listing can be published. J.A.R.V.I.S desktop is Windows/Python; Android native APK is still future work.
INSAN CREATIONS cannot complete Google’s identity checks without your login, payment, and (for Business) a verifiable address.
